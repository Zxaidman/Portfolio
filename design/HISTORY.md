# Design history

Every design the site has shipped. **No repeats:** before a new design, read this list, `design/INSPIRATION.md` and the snapshots in `design/archive/`, and choose a direction that differs clearly in name, palette, type, layout and motion from every entry. `validate` enforces name, font set and palette.

- **Minor (5:50 AM every day):** a new design system: palette themes, type, scale, components and one signature motion. Snapshot in `design/archive/`.
- **Major (5:50 AM every 3rd day, Sundays not counted):** a whole revamp with a new layout archetype, plus a Claude Design System. Snapshot in `design/archive/`.
