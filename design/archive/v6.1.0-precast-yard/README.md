# Precast yard (v6.1.0)

The coordination room is rebuilt as a precast yard: every panel is a factory-cast concrete element lifted into the wall. Panels are pale cast slabs on a darker yard ground, with a recessed lifting-anchor pocket in each top corner, a stencilled erection mark (piece code, cast date, weight) spray-painted on the title bar, and a chunky pallet-strap edge. Type is Stick No Bills, a stencil face with bridged counters like the marks sprayed on precast units, over Albert Sans and Spline Sans Mono. Buttons and chips are stencil plates: a dashed bridge line and square shoulders, filled with spray paint on press. Signature motion: crane set. As each panel scrolls into view it is lowered into place on two hoist lines from its lifting anchors, swings slightly, lands with a small settle, the lines go slack and lift away, and a grout bead fills under it.

Restore by copying these files back to the repo root, then `python3 scripts/portfolio.py build`.
